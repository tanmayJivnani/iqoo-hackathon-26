### 🟢 Slide 1: Title & Problem Hook
* **Slide Title:** Pune Tree Rakshak — Phone-First Civic Accountability & Urban Forest Preservation Platform
* **📱 What to show on Phone:** Show the **App Launch Screen / Grievance Tracker** list.

  > Today, we present **Pune Tree Rakshak**, deployed on this flagship iQOO hardware. We have built an end-to-end, phone-first accountability platform that transforms physical camera evidence into court-grade statutory notices and guarantees physical resolution with zero-trust re-audits."

### 🟢 Slide 2: Demo Step 1 — Intelligent Triage & Sensor Telemetry
* **Slide Title:** Intelligent Civic Triage & Hardware Tilt Telemetry
* **📱 What to show on Phone:** 
  1. Tap **"New Grievance"** (or plus button).
  2. Select **"Dangerous Leaning Tree"**.
  3. Tilt the phone physically in your hand to show the **live tilt sensor reading** on the screen!
* **🎙️ What to Say (35 seconds):**
  > "Watch Step 1 on the device. Instead of messy freeform descriptions, the citizen selects from 7 strict statutory categories under the Maharashtra Tree Act.
  > Notice the live sensor readout on screen: our app accesses the phone's hardware accelerometer to mathematically calculate the tree's tilt angle against true vertical. In this case, 23.4 degrees of lean.
  > We aren't guessing — we are using device physics to legally prove an imminent collapse hazard under Section 7(b). Meanwhile, our spatial engine maps the GPS coordinates directly to PMC's Ward Office jurisdiction."

### 🟢 Slide 3: Demo Step 2 — 3-Layer Evidence Standard & AI Anti-Spam Gate
* **Slide Title:** 3-Layer Forensic Evidence Standard & AI Anti-Spam Gate
* **📱 What to show on Phone:**
  1. Move into the **Camera Guidance & Capture Screen**.
  2. Point out the 3-Layer indicators: **Layer 1 (Locus Context) &rarr; Layer 2 (Structural Frame) &rarr; Layer 3 (Forensic Macro)**.
* **🎙️ What to Say (35 seconds):**
  > "Municipal officers reject over 80% of citizen photos because a single close-up lacks context.
  > We solve this with our **3-Layer Forensic Standard**:
  > 1. **Locus Context:** A wide street view proving the tree is on a public municipal footpath.
  > 2. **Structural Frame:** A mid-range frame demonstrating the lean trajectory and pedestrian hazard zone.
  > 3. **Forensic Macro:** A close-up capturing the exact violation — whether it's fresh saw teeth, iron nails, or concrete choking the root collar.
  > Crucially, our **AI Anti-Spam Gate** inspects the photos *before* municipal submission. If someone uploads a selfie, laptop keyboard, or indoor furniture, it is instantly rejected with a cooldown, protecting civic and AI resources."

1. **Pacing:** Let the slide speak for 3-5 seconds while you touch the corresponding screen on your phone.
2. **Tilt Phone:** During Slide 2, physically tilt your phone in front of the judges so they see the accelerometer angle change dynamically!
3. **Open Sealed PDF:** During Slide 5, tap to open the PDF — judges love seeing the official seal, GPS watermarks, and Marathi prayer.
