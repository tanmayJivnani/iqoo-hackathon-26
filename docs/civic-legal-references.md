# Civic notice references

Reviewed on 10 October 2026. Notices request inspection and appropriate action. They do not establish an offence, guarantee a penalty or impose a statutory response deadline.

- Solid Waste Management Rules, 2026 replaced the 2016 rules from 1 April 2026: [Ministry announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2219676&lang=2&reg=48). No blanket ₹5,000 penalty is asserted.
- Road and drainage notices reference municipal duties under Section 63 of the [Maharashtra Municipal Corporations Act, 1949](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/16071?sam_handle=123456789%2F2505).
- Pedestrian access also references the [Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2155?locale=en), Section 41. Applicable accessibility standards and jurisdiction need case-specific review.
- Noise notices reference Rules 5, 7 and 8 of the Noise Pollution (Regulation and Control) Rules, 2000, as amended. Authorities must check permissions, valid exemptions and notified silence-zone status. A citizen marking a nearby school or hospital does not establish a notified zone. The usual night window is 22:00–06:00 IST; [official noise guidance](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/PrintRelease.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=67367) describes the statutory measurement framework. Phone dBFS readings are uncalibrated digital levels and cannot be compared to dB(A) legal thresholds.
- The default police recipient, `cp.pune@mahapolice.gov.in`, comes from the [Maharashtra Police commissioner contact directory](https://www.mahapolice.gov.in/uploads/contact_email/cpcontact2.xlsx.pdf). The app requests forwarding to the jurisdictional station; it does not claim a verified local chowki match. The 112 button opens the phone dialler.

New municipal categories use configurable recipients (`PMC_ROADS_EMAIL`, `PMC_DRAINAGE_EMAIL`, `PMC_ENCROACHMENT_EMAIL`, `PMC_SWM_EMAIL`). Without these they use PMC central reception with explicit forwarding language. Contact configuration is not an automatically verified ward directory.

Photos and audio are stored with device-reported coordinates and times. Imported-photo timestamps and locations are not authenticated. The record digest identifies the saved record and is not a government evidence certificate. Fast tree notices use general Act references and request inspection rather than declaring an offence. The optional older AI tree workflow still inherits the original routing dataset and needs a separate legal review before public rollout.
